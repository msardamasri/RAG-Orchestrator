# Remote and Hybrid Work

Owner: People Operations and Workplace. Last reviewed: March 2026. Northwind Payments is a hybrid company: we believe in time together in the office and in quiet time at home.

## The hybrid model

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days, when everyone is expected in the office. Teams are free to choose the remaining days, and many agree on a shared pattern so that meetings in person are easy to plan. If you need to swap an anchor day, agree it with your manager in advance; it should be the exception, not a habit.

## Core hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the time zone of your office. Inside core hours you should be available for meetings and reply to messages in a reasonable time. Outside them you organise your day as you prefer, as long as you work your contractual hours. Avoid scheduling recurring meetings outside core hours, especially when they involve colleagues in both Europe and Texas.

## Home office support

- New joiners receive a one-off home office allowance of 500 EUR.
- The allowance is paid with the first payroll after your first 30 days at the company.
- In addition, hybrid employees receive 40 EUR per month towards home internet costs.

The allowance can be spent on a desk, a chair, lighting or similar items; you do not need to send receipts for it. Monitors, keyboards and headsets are provided by IT and should not be bought with the allowance. Within 60 days of joining, complete the ergonomics self-assessment in PeopleHub; Workplace will follow up if it flags a problem.

## Connectivity and workspace

Your home connection should offer at least 25 Mbps download speed so that video calls work reliably. Work from a private space when you discuss customer or payment data, and never take calls about customers in cafes or on trains. If you live more than 60 km from the nearest Northwind office, we reimburse a co-working desk up to 150 EUR per month.

## Working from abroad

You may work from another country for up to 30 working days per calendar year. A single stay may not be longer than 15 consecutive working days. Request it with form HR-40 (Work From Abroad Request) at least 21 days before you travel. Requests are checked by People Operations, Tax and Security, because working from some countries creates tax or data protection obligations for the company.

- Only countries on the approved list in PeopleHub are allowed.
- You must keep at least 4 hours of overlap with your office's core hours.
- Travel, accommodation and any extra insurance are at your own cost.
- Days spent at another Northwind office on a business trip do not count towards the limit.

## Fully remote contracts

Fully remote contracts are an exception and need the approval of a VP. Fully remote employees are expected to visit their home office at least once per quarter, and the company covers that travel. They receive the same allowances as hybrid employees.

## Etiquette

- Keep your calendar up to date with where you are working each day.
- Cameras are encouraged but not mandatory; nobody has to explain why theirs is off.
- Write decisions down in the relevant channel so that people who were not in the room can follow.
- Respect time zones: use scheduled send for messages outside the recipient's working day.

## When the model does not fit

Some roles, such as office management and hardware support, need more presence and have their own schedule agreed with the team lead. Temporary arrangements for health or family reasons are handled case by case by People Operations and reviewed every 3 months. Write to workplace@northwind-payments.example with any question about this document.
