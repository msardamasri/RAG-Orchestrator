# Expenses and Reimbursement

Owner: Finance. Last reviewed: February 2026. This document explains what Northwind Payments reimburses, how to claim it and why a claim may be sent back. Flights, hotels and daily allowances for business trips follow their own rules and are not covered here.

## General principles

Spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own. All claims are filed in Claimbox, our expense portal, from the web or the mobile app. Each claim needs a short business reason and the correct cost centre. Amounts in other currencies are converted automatically by Claimbox at the exchange rate of the day the expense was made.

## Limits per category

- Client meals: up to 60 EUR per person, including the Northwind hosts.
- Team meals: up to 35 EUR per person, once per quarter per team. Alcohol is not reimbursed at team meals.
- Taxis and ride-hailing: up to 45 EUR per ride. Longer rides need a note explaining why public transport was not an option.
- Learning budget: up to 400 EUR per year for books, courses and certifications.
- Software subscriptions: up to 25 EUR per month without prior approval; anything above that goes through the IT procurement queue.
- Mobile phone plan: up to 30 EUR per month for employees who are on call or customer-facing.

Gifts to customers above 50 EUR require written approval from Compliance before the purchase, because of anti-bribery rules.

## Receipts

A receipt is required for every expense above 15 EUR. A clear photo taken with the Claimbox app is acceptable, as long as it shows the date, the vendor and the tax amount. Card statements are not receipts. If a receipt is lost, fill in form FIN-09 (Missing Receipt Declaration). Each employee may use the missing receipt declaration at most 2 times per quarter. Keep paper originals for 90 days in case Finance asks for them during an audit.

## Deadlines and payment

Claims must be submitted within 45 days of the date of the expense. Claims approved by the 10th of the month are reimbursed with that month's payroll on the 25th. Claims approved later are paid with the next payroll run. If you leave the company, submit outstanding claims before your last working day.

## Approval chain

The approver depends on the total amount of the claim.

- Up to 500 EUR: your line manager.
- From 500 EUR to 2,500 EUR: your line manager and then the department head.
- Above 2,500 EUR: the CFO office gives the final approval after the department head.

Approvers have 5 working days to act. Nobody may approve their own claim, and managers may not ask a team member to file an expense on their behalf.

## Rejection codes

When Finance returns a claim, Claimbox shows one of the following codes. A returned claim can be corrected and resubmitted once.

- EXP-101: the receipt is missing or unreadable.
- EXP-204: duplicate claim, the same receipt has already been submitted.
- EXP-310: the amount is above the category limit and no pre-approval was attached.
- EXP-415: the claim was submitted after the deadline.
- EXP-502: wrong or inactive cost centre.

A claim rejected with EXP-415 can only be reopened by the department head, and only once per employee per year.

## Corporate cards

A corporate card is offered to employees who spend more than 1,000 EUR per month for three consecutive months. Card transactions still need a receipt and a business reason in Claimbox within 14 days of the transaction. Personal purchases on a corporate card are not allowed, even if you intend to pay them back. Cards with 3 unreconciled transactions are frozen until the backlog is cleared.

## What we do not reimburse

- Fines, including parking and speeding tickets.
- Commuting between home and your usual office.
- Minibar charges and in-room entertainment.
- Clothing, unless it is branded material ordered by Marketing.
