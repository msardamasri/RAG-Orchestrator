# Product Plans and Pricing

Owner: Product and Revenue Operations. Last reviewed: May 2026. This is the internal reference for the three public plans of the Northwind Payments platform. Prices exclude VAT.

## Plans at a glance

- Starter (plan code PLAN-ST) costs 49 EUR per month and includes 10,000 API calls per month.
- Growth (plan code PLAN-GR) costs 249 EUR per month and includes 100,000 API calls per month.
- Scale (plan code PLAN-SC) costs 899 EUR per month and includes 500,000 API calls per month.

Included API calls reset on the first day of each calendar month and do not roll over.

## Overage

Calls above the included volume are never blocked; they are billed at the end of the month.

- Starter overage is 0.006 EUR per additional API call.
- Growth overage is 0.004 EUR per additional API call.
- Scale overage is 0.0025 EUR per additional API call.

Customers receive an email when they reach 80% and 100% of their included calls. Calls to the sandbox environment are free and are not counted.

## Annual billing

Customers who pay for a full year up front receive a 15% discount on the plan price. With annual billing, Starter works out at 41.65 EUR per month. With annual billing, Growth works out at 211.65 EUR per month. With annual billing, Scale works out at 764.15 EUR per month. The discount applies to the plan fee only, not to overage or transaction fees. Annual plans renew automatically unless cancelled 30 days before the renewal date.

## Transaction fees

In addition to the plan fee, each successful card payment carries a transaction fee.

- Starter: 1.2% plus 0.20 EUR per successful card payment.
- Growth: 0.9% plus 0.15 EUR per successful card payment.
- Scale: 0.7% plus 0.10 EUR per successful card payment.

Failed and refunded payments carry no transaction fee. Chargebacks cost 15 EUR each on every plan.

## Features per plan

Starter includes card payments, payment links, 1 sandbox environment and 2 team seats. Starter customers receive Standard support.

Growth adds recurring billing, 3 sandbox environments and 10 team seats. Webhooks with automatic retries are available from Growth upwards. Growth customers receive Priority support.

Scale adds settlement in 12 currencies, single sign-on, a dedicated IP address and custom fraud rules. Scale has unlimited team seats. Scale customers receive Premier support.

## Free trial

Every new account starts with a free trial of 21 days on the Growth feature set. No credit card is required to start the trial. Trial accounts are limited to 1,000 live API calls. At the end of the trial the account moves to Starter unless the customer chooses another plan.

## Discounts

- Startups younger than 2 years with less than 1 million EUR in funding receive 30% off any plan for their first 12 months.
- Registered charities receive 50% off the plan fee, with standard transaction fees.

Discounts cannot be combined with each other. The annual billing discount can be combined with the charity discount but not with the startup discount. Any other discount needs written approval from Revenue Operations.

## Changing plan

Upgrades take effect immediately and the price difference is prorated for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period. A customer who exceeds their included calls for 3 months in a row is contacted by their account manager with an upgrade suggestion, but is never moved automatically.

## Invoicing and payment

Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month. Payment is due within 14 days of the invoice date. Accounts that are more than 30 days overdue are suspended until the balance is paid. Customers can pay by card or SEPA direct debit; bank transfer is available on annual plans only.

## Cancellation

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the paid month. Customer data is kept for 60 days after cancellation and then deleted, except for records that payment regulations require us to keep longer.
