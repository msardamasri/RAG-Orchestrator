# Onboarding Guide

Owner: People Operations. Last reviewed: January 2026. Welcome to Northwind Payments. This guide describes what happens before you start, during your first week and through your first months.

## Before you start

Once the contract is signed you receive a link to form HR-03 (New Hire Details), which collects your bank details, tax information and emergency contact. Please complete it at least 5 working days before your start date so that payroll and IT can prepare. You will also choose your laptop keyboard layout and tell us about any dietary needs for the welcome lunch.

## Your first week

New joiners start on Mondays, so that each group goes through the week together.

- Day 1: arrive at reception at 09:30. You collect your laptop, set up your accounts and have lunch with your team.
- Day 2: product deep dive with the Product team, including a live demo of a payment from checkout to settlement.
- Day 3: a 90-minute security and compliance session, followed by time to complete mandatory courses.
- Day 4: you spend 2 hours shadowing the customer support team to hear real customer problems.
- Day 5: you agree a 30-day plan with your manager and join the Friday all-hands, where new joiners introduce themselves.

## Accounts created for you

On day 1 IT creates your email, Slack, PeopleHub and Vaultkeep accounts. Engineers also receive access to the code repository and the staging environment. Access to production is not given in the first week; it is requested later by your manager and requires the security course to be completed. If an account is missing, ask in #it-help rather than borrowing a colleague's login.

## The buddy programme

Every new joiner is paired with a buddy from a different team. The buddy programme lasts for your first 90 days. You and your buddy meet for 30 minutes every week. The buddy is not your manager and does not evaluate you; their role is to answer the questions you might hesitate to ask elsewhere, introduce you to people and explain how things really work. Buddies volunteer for the role and receive a short briefing from People Operations.

## Mandatory courses

Within your first 30 days you must complete three courses in PeopleHub: anti-money-laundering basics, the code of conduct and data protection. Together they take about 3 hours. Your manager receives a reminder if any of them is still open after 3 weeks.

## Probation

The probation period is 6 months for employees in Barcelona and Stockholm. For employees in Austin the probation period is 90 days. During probation you have structured check-ins with your manager at 30, 60 and 90 days, each with written notes that you can read and comment on. The final probation review takes place 2 weeks before the period ends, so there are no surprises on the last day.

## Goals for engineers

Engineers are expected to ship a small change to production within their first 2 weeks, paired with a teammate. The aim is to learn the deployment pipeline early, not to deliver a big feature. Each engineering team keeps a list of starter tasks labelled "good first change".

## Goals for everyone else

By the end of the first month you should be able to explain what Northwind sells and to whom, know the people you will work with most, and have delivered one small piece of work of your own. Your 30-day plan lists these outcomes explicitly.

## Welcome kit

On your first day you will find a welcome kit on your desk: a backpack, a notebook, a water bottle and a hoodie in the size you gave us. Fully remote joiners receive the kit by courier during their first week.

## Feedback on onboarding

After 45 days you receive a short survey about the onboarding experience. We read every answer and review this guide twice a year. Questions go to onboarding@northwind-payments.example.
