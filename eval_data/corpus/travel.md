# Business Travel Policy

Owner: Finance and Workplace. Last reviewed: February 2026. This policy covers trips made on behalf of Northwind Payments: customer visits, conferences and visits to another office.

## Booking

All flights, trains and hotels are booked in TripDesk, the company travel tool. Flights should be booked at least 14 days before departure. Bookings made outside TripDesk are reimbursed only if the travel team approved them beforehand, for example when a conference offers a special hotel rate. Trips with an estimated total cost above 1,500 EUR need the approval of the department head before anything is booked.

## Flights and trains

The class of travel depends on the scheduled duration of the flight, counted per leg.

- Flights shorter than 6 hours: economy class.
- Flights between 6 and 10 hours: premium economy.
- Flights longer than 10 hours: business class, with approval from a VP.

Choose rail instead of air when the door-to-door journey by train takes less than 4 hours. First class on trains is allowed for journeys longer than 3 hours. Upgrades paid with your own money or points are fine. Loyalty points earned on business trips belong to the traveller.

## Hotels

TripDesk only shows hotels that meet our safety criteria. The maximum nightly rate, including taxes, depends on the city.

- Barcelona: 160 EUR per night.
- Stockholm: 1,900 SEK per night.
- Austin: 210 USD per night.
- London: 220 GBP per night.
- New York: 320 USD per night.

During major trade fairs prices can exceed these caps; in that case ask the travel team for an exception before you book. Staying with friends or family instead of a hotel is allowed, and you may claim a thank-you gift of up to 40 EUR per stay.

## Per diem

Instead of claiming individual meals while travelling, you receive a daily allowance.

- Europe: 55 EUR per day.
- United States: 70 USD per day.
- Any other destination: 45 EUR per day.

On the day of departure and the day of return you receive 50% of the daily allowance. If a meal is provided by a customer or included in a conference ticket, the per diem is not reduced. The per diem is added to your next payroll after the trip is closed in TripDesk.

## Ground transport

Use public transport where it is safe and practical. Rental cars are booked in the compact category unless 4 or more colleagues travel together. If you use your own car for a business trip, mileage is reimbursed at 0.26 EUR per km. Tolls and parking during a trip are reimbursed in addition to mileage.

## Conferences

Each employee may attend up to 2 conferences per year at the company's expense. Speaking at a conference does not count towards that number. Submit the conference request at least 6 weeks before the event so that early-bird tickets can be bought.

## Combining business and personal travel

You may add personal days before or after a business trip. The company pays for the flights as long as the fare is not higher than the business-only itinerary, and you cover accommodation and meals on the personal days. Personal days must still be requested as annual leave in the normal way.

## Safety and insurance

Every trip booked in TripDesk is covered automatically by the company travel insurance, including medical care and lost luggage. Save the 24-hour emergency assistance number shown on your itinerary before you leave. Trips to destinations rated high risk by our insurer need sign-off from the Security team at least 10 days before departure.

## After the trip

Close the trip in TripDesk within 7 days of returning, confirming the dates actually travelled. Cancelled or unused tickets must be reported to the travel team so that refunds or credits can be recovered. Questions go to travel@northwind-payments.example.
