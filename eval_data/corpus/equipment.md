# IT Equipment Policy

Owner: IT. Last reviewed: March 2026. This document explains what hardware Northwind Payments provides, how long you keep it and what to do when something goes wrong.

## Laptops by role

IT buys a small number of standard models so that spare parts and loaners are always available.

- Engineers and data scientists receive the Halden Book 16 Pro with 32 GB of memory.
- Designers receive the Halden Book 16 Pro with 64 GB of memory and a colour-calibrated display.
- Sales, operations and all other roles receive the Tessel Air 13 with 16 GB of memory.

Requests for a non-standard machine need a written justification from your manager and approval from the Head of IT. Laptops arrive pre-configured with encryption, the VPN client and the device management agent; do not remove any of them.

## Peripherals

Each employee can order one external monitor, one keyboard, one mouse and one headset from the IT catalogue. Headsets outside the catalogue are reimbursed up to 120 EUR. A second monitor is available for roles that need it, such as designers and support engineers. Peripherals for home use are shipped to your address at no cost.

## Refresh cycle

Laptops are replaced every 36 months. Company phones are replaced every 24 months. Monitors are replaced after 5 years or when they fail. IT contacts you about 4 weeks before your laptop reaches its refresh date to schedule the swap. After a refresh you may buy your old laptop for 15% of its original purchase price; the device is wiped and removed from management first.

## Asset tags

Every device carries a sticker with its asset tag. Laptop asset tags follow the format NW-LAP-##### where the five digits are a serial number, for example NW-LAP-04417. Monitors use the prefix NW-MON and phones use NW-PHN. Quote the asset tag in every request to IT; it lets us find the warranty, the purchase date and the assigned user in seconds. Do not remove or cover the sticker.

## Loss or theft

A lost or stolen device must be reported to IT within 4 hours, at any time of day. Call the IT emergency line or write to it-help@northwind-payments.example; IT then locks the device and triggers a remote wipe. Afterwards, complete form IT-12 (Lost Device Report) within 2 working days. In case of theft, file a police report within 48 hours and attach a copy to the form.

You are not charged for a first incident. A second loss within 24 months may lead to a contribution of up to 300 EUR, decided by your department head. A replacement laptop is normally ready within 2 working days.

## Repairs and loaners

If your laptop is damaged or faulty, open a ticket in #it-help with the asset tag and a description of the problem. A loaner laptop is provided within 1 working day while yours is repaired. Do not take company devices to third-party repair shops; warranty and data protection both require that repairs go through IT.

## Software

Install software from the self-service catalogue whenever possible. Licences for tools outside the catalogue are requested through the IT procurement queue, and IT answers within 5 working days. Unlicensed or pirated software is never allowed on company devices.

## Travelling with equipment

Carry laptops as hand luggage and never leave them in a parked car. For trips to destinations that IT classifies as high risk, you receive a clean travel laptop instead of your usual machine; request it at least 5 working days before departure.

## Leaving the company

All equipment must be returned within 10 working days of your last working day. Remote employees receive a prepaid courier label. Peripherals worth less than 50 EUR, such as mice and cables, do not need to be returned.

## Contact

IT support is available in #it-help from 08:00 to 19:00 Central European Time on working days, with an emergency line for lost devices outside those hours.
