# Leave Policy

Owner: People Operations. Last reviewed: January 2026. This policy applies to all permanent employees of Northwind Payments in Barcelona, Stockholm and Austin. Contractors and interns should check their own agreement.

## Annual leave entitlement

Paid annual leave depends on the office your contract is attached to, not on where you happen to be working on a given day.

- Barcelona: 23 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Stockholm: 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Austin: 20 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Employees earn 1 additional day of annual leave for every 3 full years of service, up to a maximum of 5 additional days. People who join during the year receive a pro-rated entitlement, rounded up to the nearest half day. Public holidays are not deducted from annual leave; the calendar of public holidays for each office is published as a separate document every December.

## Carry-over

We want people to actually rest, so leave should be taken in the year in which it is earned. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March, after which they are forfeited. Unused leave is never paid out in cash, except where local law requires it when a contract ends.

## How to request leave

All annual leave is requested in PeopleHub using form HR-17 (Leave Request). The form asks for the dates, the type of leave and the name of the colleague who will cover urgent matters while you are away.

- Absences of more than 3 working days need at least 10 working days of notice.
- Shorter absences need at least 2 working days of notice.
- Your line manager must approve or decline the request within 3 working days.

If the manager does not reply in that time, the request escalates automatically to the department head. A manager can decline a request only for documented business reasons and must propose alternative dates. As a planning rule, no more than one third of a team should be on annual leave at the same time. Do not pay for non-refundable trips before the form shows the status "Approved".

## Sick leave

If you are unwell, tell your manager before 09:30 local time on the first day of absence; a chat message is enough. A medical certificate is required from day 3 of a continuous absence, and it is uploaded with form HR-22 (Medical Certificate Upload). Northwind pays full salary for the first 15 sick days in a calendar year and 75% of salary from day 16 onwards, unless local law is more generous. Sick days that fall inside booked annual leave are credited back once a certificate has been provided.

## Parental leave

Northwind offers the same parental leave in all three offices, on top of whatever the local social security system provides.

- Primary caregivers receive 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
- Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave.

Parental leave must start within 12 months of the birth or adoption and may be split into at most 2 blocks. Tell People Operations at least 8 weeks before the expected start date using form HR-31 (Parental Leave Notice). During the first 4 weeks back at work, returning parents may work 80% of their hours at full pay.

## Other types of leave

- Bereavement: 4 paid days for the loss of a close family member and 1 paid day for other relatives.
- Moving house: 1 paid day, once per calendar year.
- Volunteering: 2 paid days per year for work with a registered charity.
- Civic duties such as jury service: paid for the duration stated in the official summons.
- Unpaid leave: up to 30 calendar days per year, approved by the department head and People Operations.

Requests for these types of leave also go through PeopleHub, choosing the matching leave type in the form. For bereavement leave you can file the request after the fact; nobody expects paperwork on a day like that.
